# Josie Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 14, 2026

Josie is a desktop app for managing coding-agent sessions, published by Douglas Trojanowski ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information Josie collects, why, and the choices you have.

## The short version

- Your code, files, terminal output, prompts and agent conversations **stay on your computer**. We don't collect them.
- If you **sign in**, we receive your account details and information about how you use Josie. We use these to understand which features are used, improve Josie, and give you support.
- You can use Josie without signing in.
- We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

## Information we collect

### Account information (when you sign in)

When you create an account or sign in, we receive:

- your email address
- your name, if you sign in with Google or add one
- your Google account ID, if you sign in with Google
- your password, if you use email and password. It's handled by our sign-in provider and stored only in hashed form; we never see it.

### Usage information (when you're signed in)

While you're signed in, Josie may send us information about how you use the app, linked to your account, such as:

- which features and screens you use, and how often
- when you open and close Josie, and how long you use it
- the Josie version, Windows version and basic device information
- errors and crashes Josie runs into

Usage information **doesn't include** the contents of your code, files, terminal sessions, prompts, agent conversations, ideas or tasks, or the names and paths of your projects.

### Support information

If you contact us, we keep what you send, such as your email address, your message, and any screenshots or files you choose to attach.

### Update checks

Josie checks GitHub for new versions. Like any web request, this exposes your IP address and basic request information to GitHub, under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). We don't receive this information.

## Information that stays on your computer

Josie stores its settings, session history, ideas, tasks, layouts and logs in its data folder on your computer. This data isn't sent to us. Your sign-in is kept there too, encrypted with Windows' built-in protection.

## How we use information

- to provide your account and keep you signed in
- to understand how Josie is used, so we can fix problems and decide what to improve
- to answer your questions and give you support
- to keep Josie secure and prevent misuse
- to tell you about important changes to Josie or this policy

## Services that handle information for us

- **Google Firebase Authentication** handles sign-in and stores account information ([Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)).
- **Google Sign-In**, if you choose to continue with Google.
- **GitHub**, which hosts Josie's downloads and updates.
- Any service we use to receive usage information or support messages. They process it only on our behalf.

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with advertisers. We may disclose information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or to a successor if Josie is transferred to a new owner.

## Tools you connect

Josie runs tools you install yourself, such as Claude Code, Codex and Git. They connect to their own services under their own privacy policies; we don't receive what they send.

One optional feature is the exception: if you turn on **Check my Claude plan usage**, Josie uses Claude Code's saved sign-in to ask Anthropic for your plan usage and shows it to you. That request goes directly from your computer to Anthropic, and we don't receive the result.

## How long we keep information

We keep account information while your account exists. We keep usage information for as long as it's useful for improving Josie, and support messages for as long as needed to help you. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your account and usage information within a reasonable time, except where we need to keep it by law.

## Your choices

- **Don't sign in.** Josie works without an account, and we collect no account or usage information from you.
- **Sign out** at any time in Settings → Account. Josie stops sending usage information until you sign in again.
- **See, correct or delete your information.** Email us and we'll respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under local law, such as the right to a copy of your data, and we'll honor them.
- **Erase local data** with Josie's own data controls, or by uninstalling Josie and deleting its data folder.

## Security

We protect information with measures such as encrypted connections and encrypted local storage of your sign-in. No system is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

## Children

Josie isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

## Where information is processed

We're based in the United States, and our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We'll change the effective date above, and for significant changes we'll let you know in Josie or by email.
